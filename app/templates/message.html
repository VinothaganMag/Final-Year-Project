🚩 Phishing Message Patterns
  • ⛔ Urgency ("Act now", "Limited time")
  • ⛔ Requests for credentials or personal info
  • ⛔ Generic greetings ("Dear customer")
  • ⛔ Suspicious links or attachments
  • ⛔ Prize / lottery announcements
  • ⛔ Threats about account suspension
✅ What To Do
  • ✔ Don't click links in suspicious messages
  • ✔ Verify sender identity independently
  • ✔ Contact the company via official channels
  • ✔ Report phishing to your email provider
  • ✔ Enable two-factor authentication
  • ✔ Keep software and browsers updated
